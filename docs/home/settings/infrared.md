# Infrared

**Ubo App**  
Home screen actions → Menu → Settings → Hardware → Infrared

![Infrared](../../assets/images/settings/infrared.png)

**Infrared** (󰻅) lets you pair and manage IR remote devices and control whether the device receives or propagates keypad actions from IR. Open it from [Settings](../settings.md) → **Hardware**.

## What you see

When you open **Infrared**, the screen title is **Infrared** and you see:

- **[Replay Devices](infrared-replay-devices.md)** (󰑔) — List of registered IR devices. Select a device to send (replay) its IR code. If none are registered, you see “No devices registered”.
- **[Manage Keys](infrared-manage-keys.md)** (󰻅) — Add or remove IR devices:
  - **Add Keys** (+) — Start pairing a new remote. You may be prompted to point the remote at the device and press a button to capture codes.
  - **Remove Keys** (-) — List of registered devices; select one to remove it. Shows “No devices registered” if the list is empty.
- **[Settings](infrared-settings.md)** () — Toggles for IR behaviour:
  - **Receive Keys** — When on, the device accepts IR keypresses (e.g. from a remote). When off, IR input is ignored.
  - **Propagate Keys** — When on, keypad actions are propagated (e.g. to other systems). When off, they are not. The selected state is highlighted.

## Navigation

- Use **UP** and **DOWN** to move between options and (in sub-menus) between devices or settings.
- Use **L1**, **L2**, or **L3** to select an option, choose a device, or toggle a setting.
- Use **BACK** to go back one level or leave Infrared.
- Use **HOME** to return to the home screen.

During device registration, **BACK** or **HOME** may cancel the pairing process.
